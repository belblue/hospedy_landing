Usage Guidelines

✓ Do

Use the full color logo on light or white backgrounds

✓ Do

Use the dark mode variant on dark backgrounds (#1a1a2e or darker)

✓ Do

Maintain minimum clear space equal to the door width around the logo

✗ Don't

Stretch, rotate, or distort the logo proportions

✗ Don't

Change the logo colors outside of approved variants

✗ Don't

Place the logo on busy backgrounds without sufficient contrast
